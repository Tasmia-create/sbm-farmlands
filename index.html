About SBM Farmlands

A Different Way to Own Farmland

SBM Farmlands is a managed farmland ownership project near Bagepalli along the NH-44 corridor. It offers individuals and families a straightforward way to own real agricultural land—without having to personally manage day-to-day farming.

Every plot starting from 5,000 sq. ft. is registered directly in your name while our team handles daily agricultural upkeep. You can choose your preferred crops or fruit orchards, and harvest proceeds are shared according to the agreed management arrangement.

Enjoy shared cottage stays for weekends, planned clubhouse amenities, and the option to build a custom farmhouse—with a long-term project vision expanding up to 50 acres.

Curious to see how it works? Come see the farmlands for yourself.

The Managed Farmland Concept

You Own the Land.
We Manage the Farming.

Enjoy hassle-free farmland ownership while living in the city. We care for the soil, crops, and harvests so your land thrives year-round.

01

Choose Your Farmland

Select your farmland plot starting from 5,000 sq. ft. and above. The land is registered in your name.

02

Choose What Grows

Discuss what you would like to grow—fruit orchards, crops, vegetables, Sandalwood, Mahogany, or other suitable agricultural cultivation.

03

SBM Manages The Farmland

SBM manages the agricultural upkeep and cultivation of your farmland, handling irrigation, soil health, and routine farming operations.

04

Harvest & Proceeds

When produce is harvested and sold, applicable proceeds are given to the owner according to the agreed management arrangement.

The SBM team will walk you through cultivation, management, and the agreed arrangement in a simple, transparent way.
